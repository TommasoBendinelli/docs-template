\docTitle{How to use coding agents}

Codex must be used exclusively from the MacBook computerl, and ideally from \code{/Users/tbe/Documents/docs}.
Secrets needed for codex are available at \code{.env}, in the future we are planning to have this file encrypted and to ensure that codex cannot just descript it.
In the past we tried to used \code{agent-vault}, however we stopped since it had too many moving components.

\paragraph{Saving conversations}
Conversations are saved by openai in \code{.codex/sessions} and \code{.codex/archived_conversations}.
Addittionally, conversation are also backed up in \code{~/agentic_conversations/agentic_sessions/sessions} which is also synced with tommaso-dekstop for redundancy
